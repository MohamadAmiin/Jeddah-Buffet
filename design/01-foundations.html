matcami · design system

Foundations

Everything a matcami screen is allowed to be made of — three surfaces, one palette, six type roles, one money rule, one status vocabulary, and a touch scale sized for someone standing at a counter.

What this is. The reference sheet a developer opens before writing a component. Every element below is drawn with a token from tokens.css — there is not one literal colour in this file. The rule it demonstrates: a component names a role, never a colour, which is what lets the same markup render correctly on the light page, the dark page and the permanently dark POS shell.

01 · surfaces

Three states, not two

The card below is written once — background: var(--c-raise), color: var(--c-ink), border-color: var(--c-line) — and rendered three times. The token names are identical in all three states; only their values differ. That is the whole contract.

02 · palette

Four grounds, seven inks

The grounds sit deliberately close together — a till screen read at arm's length does not want contrast fighting the money for attention. Which means the border, not the fill, is what separates a control from its surroundings.

Grounds

Aa--c-bg page ground
Aa--c-bg-2 recessed wells, table heads
Aa--c-raise cards, controls, the check
Aa--c-raise-2inside a card: totals, headers

Inks

Each rendered on --c-raise, the ground they are specified against. Every pair here clears 4.5:1 in all three states.

Aa --c-ink primary text
Aa --c-ink-2 secondary, labels
Aa --c-ink-3 tertiary + control borders
Aa--c-accent links, focus ring, occupied
Aa --c-ok online, sent, paid
Aa --c-warn offline, billed, needs review
Aa--c-danger void, refund, negative money

The four re-tuned values

Four inks were moved after every pair was recomputed by WCAG relative luminance. They are listed because a designer looking at the palette will otherwise assume they drifted by accident.

  • --c-ink-3 Light. The previous value measured 4.35:1 on --c-bg and 4.03:1 on --c-bg-2 — it failed on both grounds. Darkened until the tighter of the two clears; that pair, at 4.55:1, is now the tightest in the whole light state.
  • --c-ink-3 Dark. Lightened for the same reason in the other direction — tertiary text sat under the floor on the two darkest grounds.
  • --c-ok Dark. Lightened so that "sent" and "paid" green clears 4.5:1 against every status ground it is ever painted on, not just the card ground.
  • --c-danger Dark. Lightened so a void, a refund and a negative amount stay legible on --c-danger-bg — the one colour on the screen a cashier must never misread.
03 · type

Six roles, each one complete

One name carries size, line-height, tracking and weight together. You never set a font-size next to a line-height by hand; you pick the role, and the role is already tuned.

.t-displayArchivo 700 · clamp 30–46px · lh 1.08 · −.022em Xawaash Kitchen
.t-titleArchivo 600 · clamp 21–27px · lh 1.18 · −.014em Tonight's service
.t-sectionArchivo 600 · 16px · lh 1.4 · −.006em Guest check · table 4
.t-bodyPlex Sans 400 · 15px · lh 1.6 · max 68ch

Adding an item to a billed order re-opens it. Re-opening one that has already been paid needs the owner's PIN, and writes new records rather than rewinding the posted ones.

.t-captionPlex Sans 400 · 13px · lh 1.45 · --c-ink-2 Business day 14 Sep · POS1 · cashier Amina
.t-eyebrowPlex Mono 500 · 11px · .16em · uppercase Session open

Archivo for display, IBM Plex Sans for body — drawn for an equipment maker, which is the right provenance for a till. IBM Plex Mono carries all money, quantities, account codes, invoice numbers and receipt previews.

A negative carries a leading − (U+2212, not a hyphen) and --c-danger — never colour alone. Every string above is display only: the UI does no money arithmetic and does no rounding. Cents are integers, one rounding rule lives in one server function, and each line renders the price and the tax rate stored on that line, so a later menu change cannot move a past sale.

Noted while building this sheet: tokens.css ships .mark-* and .pill-* for new, sent, voided, billed, paid and offline. Four classes worth adding upstream.

06 · controls

Buttons, fields, and the border that matters

Press Tab to walk this section — every interactive element takes a 2px --c-ring outline at a 2px offset. Removing a focus outline without replacing it is an accessibility regression, not a style choice.

Buttons

A control disabled by a non-empty sync queue must say why rather than sit dead — “Close session” above would read “blocked · 3 unsynced” in place of its label on the real screen.

Two borders, one of which is not a border

--c-control-line 5.78:1 on --c-raise The boundary of anything you can touch, type into or press. WCAG 1.4.11 wants 3:1; this clears it comfortably.
--c-line 1.58:1 on --c-raise Decorative only — a divider, a card edge. Legal there, and never legal around an input. Using it on controls is the single biggest reason a screen reads as washed out.
07 · elevation & radius

Three rungs, three radii

The ladder is declared per theme, because the light rungs carry a white top highlight that is simply wrong on a dark ground. Radius is how a thing behaves, not how big it is: a control, a card, a sheet.

Elevation

--c-shadow-flat rows, inputs, anything resting on the page
--c-shadow-raised cards, the guest check, popovers
--c-shadow-floating approval dialogs, reason-code sheets

Radius

--radius-control 0.5rem · buttons, inputs, keys
--radius-card 0.75rem · cards, panels, tables
--radius-sheet 1rem · modals and sheets
08 · touch

Sized for someone standing up

Shown on the POS surface, because that is the only place this scale applies — the dashboard is seated, mouse-driven work and uses Tailwind's default density. Press one: the targets are real, not pictures of targets.

Apple — 44pt minimum44px · too small here
Material — 48dp minimum48px · too small here

Apple's 44pt and Material's 48dp assume a seated user holding the device. Touch-accuracy research puts the floor near 9.2mm with the error rate flattening around 10mm, and standing-kiosk guidance goes to 20mm — so both platform minimums sit below matcami's floor, drawn dashed above for comparison. Keep at least gap-2 between keys as well: spacing reduces mis-taps independently of size. And verify in millimetres on the real hardware, never in pixels in a browser.
