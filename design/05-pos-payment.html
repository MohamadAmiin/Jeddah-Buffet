What this demonstrates. Degraded state is permanent chrome, never a toast: the unsynced count sits in the status bar and again in the footer, where it disables session close with a stated reason. Card and mobile money disable before the tap rather than failing after it. Every figure is a display string formatted by the money module — the screen does no arithmetic and no rounding. The POS surface is light and pinned in both themes; what makes it a device rather than page chrome is the deeper ground, the density, and a drawn edge on every key.

What this screen is proving

Invariant 5 · spec 6

Fail closed, before the tap

A card tender that is accepted and then fails leaves a cashier holding a card and an order in an undefined state. Disabling the control with a written reason costs one label and removes the failure mode entirely.

Invariant 5 · spec 6

The queue outranks the shift

Unsynced sales are the only data on the device that exists nowhere else. Close session and logout stay blocked until the queue drains, and the count is chrome in two places — a missed toast is indistinguishable from one that never fired.

Invariants 1 · 7 · spec 17

The screen never does arithmetic

Every figure here is a display string from one formatter: integer cents in, a string out. One rounding rule in one function, shared by POS, server and reports. Change due included — it is mocked here and computed there.

WCAG 1.4.11

A white key needs a drawn edge

The till ground is deeper than the dashboard’s so a white key reads as raised — but that key measures only 1.22:1 against it. Elevation cannot carry a boundary that needs 3:1, so every pressable surface takes a --c-control-line border at 4.73:1.
